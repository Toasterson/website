# Redesign after the printed illumos books

Branch `redesign/phoenix-2026-10` → `main`. Review only; do not merge or
deploy without the board's say.

## Why

The printed illumos books share one visual language: a painted phoenix in
warm oranges and reds, one bold colour field per cover, quiet type, lots of
space. The phoenix is also illumos's mascot. Taking the site there puts OIDA
visibly inside the illumos community, next to illumos.org, the books and
openindiana.org, instead of the generic blue of the first version.

## What

- A phoenix palette (deep flame, flame, ember on warm paper) as the brand core;
  the dark scheme is the debugger cover's black with the same accents.
- One colour field per section, each from a cover: DTrace mint with its stripes
  and beam of light for *What we do*, drivers blue/violet for *Finances*,
  debugger black for the footer and the `pkg info` card. The stripes run along
  the top of every page and stand beside the bylaws title like a spine.
- An original, abstract phoenix (SVG generated by `scripts/phoenix.py`, no
  raster, inlined) in the hero and as a faint ember in the footer.
- A logo slot (`Logo.astro`) that keeps the text wordmark until the brand map
  decides the logo, and an illustration slot (`Illustration.astro`) so artwork
  can be swapped in one place.
- Unchanged: IBM Plex, self-hosted; the bylaws text, byte for byte; the
  build-time Open Collective and Mastodon fetches.

Principles kept: static, no JavaScript sent to visitors, no cookies, no
third-party loads, WCAG AA contrast in both schemes (checked by
`scripts/contrast.py`), works without images and with reduced motion,
MPL-2.0.

## Open points for the board

1. **Cover artwork.** The paintings on the printed books are not in the books'
   source, not on illumos.org, and no artist or licence is published anywhere I
   could find. The site therefore uses its own phoenix. If the artist and
   licence are known and permit it, the originals can go into the `hero` slot
   with attribution.
2. **Brand tickets.** The brand repository's wayfinder map has no open issues
   as of 13 October 2026, so there was nothing to align with beyond "no new
   logo". The logo slot is ready for the outcome; the favicon is still a
   placeholder letter.
3. The illumos phoenix logo itself is trademarked (use beyond "powered by"
   badges needs approval), so it is deliberately not used.
