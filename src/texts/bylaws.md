## 1. Name

The OpenIndiana and illumos Community coordination Project is an Association in terms of Art 60-79 of the swiss civil code with domicile in Niederweningen/ZH, Switzerland.

## 2. Purpose

Purpose of the association is to organize funding and Community actions for illumos and the OpenIndiana Distribution of illumos.

## 3. Activity

The association tries to reach its goals by:

**3.1.** Maintaining services and Computers used by the projects to perform their tasks.

**3.2.** Running Fundraisers or Rasing funds from Donors or Grants.

**3.3.** Archiving and Maintaining Information about the System and it's ancestory.

**3.4.** Spreading awareness of illumos and OpenIndiana.

**3.5.** Organising projects to fund and develop improvements to illumos and OpenIndiana

## 4. Membership

**4.1.** Both Individuals and Judicial persons can join the association.

**4.2.** Any individual can apply for membership

**4.3.** For Judicial persons a membership fee is agreed upon between the Board and the Judicial person wanting to join.

**4.4.** The membership ends when the member exits the Association, gets expelled from it or dies.

**4.5.** A Member can be expelled from the Association at any time by the Board. The member can take its case to the General Assembly which can overrule the Board.

## 5. Organisation

The elements of the association are:

**1.** The General Assembly

**2.** The board

The accounting year is equal to the calendar year.

## 6. The General Assembly

The general assembly is called up by the board at least once a year. Every member has the right to bring in proposals. Proposals received 14 days before the meeting will be addressed.

The invitation to the assembly including the list of points to be addressed has to be sent at least 14 days before the meeting.

Each member has one vote. The general assembly makes decisions based on the majority of votes cast. Electronic votes or votes by letter are permitted. The Board will give instructions on how members should Identify themselves to the Board for their vote to be permitted.

In the General Assembly the following topics may be discussed if needed:

- Budget for groups from the existing funds.
- Kind, Amount and regularity of Membership fees.
- Expulsion of Members
- Changes to the Bylaws
- Termination of the Association

The General Assembly votes with a qualified Majority of 2/3 of casted votes.

The General Assembly can change the topic list and add new topics to that list.

## 7. The Board

Board members are elected by the General Assembly. The term of office is one year, re-election is possible. The board consists of at least a treasurer and a secretary.

The board must respect the interests of each interest group.

The president must represent the association.

The board constitutes itself.

The association is legally represented by a signature of a board member.

The board is quorate if three or half of the board members are present, whichever is more. The board makes decisions on the majority of the present members. The president decides in case of a tie.

## 8. Changing the Bylaws

The bylaws can be changed at the General Assembly and require a two-thirds majority of the cast votes.

## 9. Annulment of the Association

The annulment of the association and the disposal of the association funds require a two-thirds majority at the General Assembly.

## 10. Signature and Liability

- The Board of Directors is authorized to sign if it does so on behalf of the General Assembly.
- At least two members of the Board of Directors must always sign.
- Only the Association's assets are liable for the Association's debts. Personal liability of members is excluded.

## 11. Final Provisions

These bylaws become effective on the 04th October 2025.
